 your goal is to take Modern Physics next year, I think the best way forward in your situation would be to take the AP Physics C course at PRISMS. That's the most concrete way that you can demonstrate mastery of knowledge and understanding of practical applications, especially since you have not completed the E&M portion of AP Physics C. From a school's perspective, the best way for the school to guarantee that a student is prepared for their upper-level classes is if that student takes the prerequisite class. That way, the student is guaranteed to develop the knowledge foundation that is desired by the upper-level course. 

Students often claim they are prepared for upper-level classes through a variety of alternatives, but the variance in content makes for inconsistent preparation. It's the reason why college faculty hate it when students are placed into upper-level classes because of AP credits. AP classes are taught with great variance, and a 5 on an AP exam could mean mastery of as low as 60% of the content. So, college faculty are very frustrated when this happens, and this frustration is no different at a high school like ours. 

Ultimately, my recommendation is to take AP Physics C. It might feel slower at first because of the review, but you'll reap the benefits if you have the patience. You'll also have a slightly easier course, which will make your adjustment to PRISMS a bit easier, especially if you have other courses that are demanding more of your time. And if you have the time, you can always ask Dr. Huang for enrichment material.
